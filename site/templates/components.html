• We posit that accurate and generalizable retargeting requires foundational priors that capture both the semantic intent and physical requirements of a motion.
  • We use object-trajectory tracking to define the interaction goal, while allowing the robot to adapt its posture, contacts and control. Using off-the-shelf models and simulation feedback, HuMoTAR reduces mean object-tracking error by 28.6% over a single Codex agent across 40 HUMOTO and OMOMO tasks.
  • Related experience helps complete the shelf lift. Reports from other furniture tasks reduce mean ADD from 23.35 to 7.77 cm under equal refinement budgets.
  • Early stopping offers a shorter refinement budget. A retrospective cutoff at 22.3 minutes retains 88.4% of the full-budget ADD reduction on average.
