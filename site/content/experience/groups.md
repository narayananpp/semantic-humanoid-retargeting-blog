## Two kinds of experience

**Similar tasks.** Five furniture interactions involving carrying, lifting, righting, pulling and pushing. The reports often address similar problems: keeping a grasp and moving the body to stay within reach.

**Randomly grouped tasks.** Five tasks drawn from the full HUMOTO catalog without matching objects or actions. This group includes foot tilting, tub dragging, drinking and two bowl-carrying tasks. Some reports are still relevant to each other.
