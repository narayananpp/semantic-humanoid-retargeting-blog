## Results with and without experience

Figure 8 compares the final reference motion outputs after equal refinement budgets, with and without experience. Each point averages all five tasks in its group; the arrow shows how that average changes when experience is supplied.

::: component experience-result-figure

With experience, average ADD and position error are lower in both groups, with a larger reduction for similar tasks. We hypothesize that prior experience is more useful when it addresses contact and coordination problems shared with the current task. Randomly grouped experience provides a smaller benefit, suggesting that the relevance of the earlier interactions matters.
