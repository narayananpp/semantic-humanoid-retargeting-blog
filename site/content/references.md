## References {#reference-list}

1. **HOI-Retarget.** Jihwan Shin et al. *HOI-Retarget: Contact-Centric Retargeting for Human-Object Interaction.* 2026. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34674) · [Project](https://shinben0327.github.io/hoi-retarget/).
2. **GMR.** Joao Pedro Araujo et al. *Retargeting Matters: General Motion Retargeting for Humanoid Motion Tracking.* 2025. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02252) · [Code](https://github.com/YanjieZe/GMR).
3. **SPIDER.** Chaoyi Pan et al. *SPIDER: Scalable Physics-Informed Dexterous Retargeting.* 2025; revised 2026. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.09484) · [Code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/spider).
4. **SONIC.** Zhengyi Luo et al. *SONIC: Supersizing Motion Tracking for Natural Humanoid Whole-Body Control.* 2025 preprint; Science Robotics, 2026. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07820) · [Project](https://nvlabs.github.io/GEAR-SONIC/).
5. **HUMOTO.** Jiaxin Lu et al. *HUMOTO: A 4D Dataset of Mocap Human Object Interactions.* 2025. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.10414) · [Project](https://jiaxin-lu.github.io/humoto/).
6. **OMOMO.** Jiaman Li, Jiajun Wu and C. Karen Liu. *Object Motion Guided Human Motion Synthesis.* SIGGRAPH Asia, 2023. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.16237) · [Project and dataset](https://lijiaman.github.io/projects/omomo/).
7. **MuJoCo.** Emanuel Todorov, Tom Erez and Yuval Tassa. *MuJoCo: A physics engine for model-based control.* IROS, 2012. [Paper](https://roboti.us/lab/papers/TodorovIROS12.pdf) · [Current simulator](https://mujoco.org/).
8. **SMPL-X.** Georgios Pavlakos et al. *Expressive Body Capture: 3D Hands, Face, and Body from a Single Image.* CVPR, 2019. [Paper and model](https://smpl-x.is.tue.mpg.de/).
9. **GPT-6 Astra.** OpenAI. *GPT-6 Astra: A new generation of intelligence.* [Model announcement](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/).
10. **Codex.** OpenAI. *Codex CLI.* [Official coding-agent repository](https://github.com/openai/codex). This citation refers to the agent software; GPT-6 Astra is the model used in our experiments.
11. **G1 and Dex3.** Unitree Robotics. [G1 platform](https://www.unitree.com/g1/) · [Official robot simulation models](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_mujoco).
